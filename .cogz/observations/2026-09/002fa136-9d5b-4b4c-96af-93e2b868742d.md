In hybrid.rs the fused code/knowledge lists are max-normalized to [0,1] then scaled by detect_proportions output. Consequence: the best knowledge hit can never outscore knowledge_prop — with code_prop=0.7, a mediocre #2 code result (0.63) beats a perfect knowledge result (0.30 ceiling). Representation is guaranteed (deliberate) but cross-list ordering is rank-position-based, not merit-based. Plausible contributor to P@5=0.637 in the 2026-09-05 benchmark. Design decision — evaluate against merit-blend before changing. Verified 2026-09-14. Backlog item 28.
