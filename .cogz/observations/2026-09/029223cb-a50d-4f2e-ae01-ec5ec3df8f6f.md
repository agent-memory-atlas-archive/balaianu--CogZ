migrate_v3 reads all (entity_id, embedding, type) rows via row_iter.filter_map(|r| r.ok()).collect() — unparseable rows are silently discarded. There is no repair path: embed_synced only embeds synced_entity_ids (created/updated entities), and a dropped row leaves content_hash unchanged so the entity is skipped on every subsequent sync — permanently absent from vector search with zero signal. The collect-to-Vec is also unbounded (fine at current scale). Verified 2026-09-14. Backlog item 25.
