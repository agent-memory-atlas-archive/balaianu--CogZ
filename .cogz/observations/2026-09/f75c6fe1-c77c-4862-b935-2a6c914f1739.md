Storage::open opens `.cogz/.lock` with `.create(true).write(true).truncate(true).open(...).ok()` — a failed open (permissions, read-only mount) yields lock_file=None and file_lock() silently becomes in-process-only. Cross-process write serialization is off with no warning, violating the "logs a warning and continues" contract in docs/design/degradation.md. `.truncate(true)` is also unnecessary — flock operates on the inode, not content. Verified 2026-09-14. Backlog item 24.
