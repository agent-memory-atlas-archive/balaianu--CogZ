All ureq::get(...).call() sites — ORT archive download + SHA256SUMS fetch in src/embed/runtime.rs and update checks in src/update.rs — use ureq's default config with no timeout. A stalled/hung connection blocks indefinitely: ensure_ort hanging means the first embed call (and thus `cogz index` or a synchronous hook) hangs with no error. On a box where hooks run synchronously this could stall every prompt_submit. Verified 2026-09-14. Backlog item 26.
