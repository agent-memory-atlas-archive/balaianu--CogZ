Benchmarked the current NLI model (deberta-v3-xsmall, quantized) vs
laurer-large-anli on 42 labeled premise/hypothesis pairs built from
CogZ's own AGENTS.md rules (`benchmark/nli_compare/`, run 2026-09-28).

Results:
- xsmall: 21/42 accuracy, p50=34ms/pair, +387MB RSS at load
- laurer-large: 14/42 accuracy (chance level), p50=229ms (~7x),
  +943MB RSS (~2.4x). Predicted "neutral" on ~76% of pairs — ANLI
  training biases it to abstain on spec-style text.

Decision input: keep deberta-v3-xsmall; laurer-large is worse on every
axis. But note the ceiling — xsmall only reaches 50% on this domain.
Contradiction recall ~57% (12/16) with precision ~0.75: it catches
direct negation/order flips but misses most entailments (paraphrases
of the same fact read as "neutral" at 0.8-1.0 confidence). Generic
NLI is weak on rule/spec text; contradiction detection will silently
miss roughly half of real contradictions between observations/rules.
