RRF fusion in search uses configurable weights: fts_weight=0.4 and vec_weight=0.6 from config.toml. The RRF constant k=60 controls the score compression. The `fuse()` function in `src/search/rrf.rs` implements the fusion formula: `score = weight / (k + rank)`. In FTS-only mode, the fts_weight is used directly as the score multiplier, bypassing the RRF formula entirely.
