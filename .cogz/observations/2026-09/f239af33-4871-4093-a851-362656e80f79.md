PRF v2 measured flat and reverted. Two changes evaluated: (a) KNN-sourced feedback pool in addition to FTS results, (b) df≥1 for title-intersection terms (vs df≥2). Effect: +.010 R@20 on clap alone, flat on httpx/cobra — at the noise floor the prior ±.003 guard was meant to catch. Reverted to FTS-only mining with df≥2 for both term sources.

The structural reason (first principles): PRF enriches vocabulary between query and corpus, but commit vocabulary lives in *git history*, not the code corpus — feedback docs that lack the word can't supply it. Anchor-free recall can't come from anchored feedback. This was the same trap as the file-level co-change variant: borrowing vocabulary from a corpus that doesn't contain it.

PRF remains on its original contract: FTS-feedback-only, df≥2, expansion-kind output, gated to pool ≤ limit.
