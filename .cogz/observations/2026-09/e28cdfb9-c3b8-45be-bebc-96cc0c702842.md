normalize_content (hand-rolled comment stripper for content hashing) treats `'a`-style lifetime annotations as char-literal opens — `fn foo<'a>(x: &'a str)` opens "string mode" at the first `'`, consuming until the next `'` or EOF. Comments inside that span are not stripped, so cosmetic comment edits DO change the hash for any entity containing lifetimes (extremely common in Rust — including CogZ itself). Also unhandled: Go backtick strings (a `//` inside `` ` `` is wrongly stripped → missed content changes), Rust raw strings `r##"..."##`, unterminated quotes running to EOF. Verified 2026-09-14.
