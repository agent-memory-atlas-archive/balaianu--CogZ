Item 94 implemented `volatility: living|record` as a pure function of
(type, properties) rather than a schema column — `category` and any
explicit override already ride `properties`, so derived-at-read is the
same rebuildability class as `entity_drift` and needs no migration.

Three live-corpus findings from the first index under the new rules:

1. Self-corpus drift rows dropped 12→6 — half the tracked drift was on
   record-class entities (observations, decisions) and drained.
2. A stray `decision` (singular) category exists beside `decisions`
   (plural) — both now classify record. Category spelling is a soft
   convention; nothing enforces it.
3. 36 legacy stale entities carry NO `stale_reason` at all — they
   predate the field. Heal correctly leaves them: reasonless stale is
   indistinguishable from a manual verdict, and silently reactivating
   records someone deliberately marked would be worse than leaving
   honest stale. If a one-time sweep is wanted it needs an explicit
   `cogz verify`/`reject` pass, not a silent heal.
